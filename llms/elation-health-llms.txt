## Core Developer

### Core Developer

- [Core Developer](https://help.elationhealth.com/core-developer.md): Core Developer is an add-on to your Elation subscription that gives your practice the APIs, Webhooks, and Hosted Database to build custom integrations, automate workflows, and run your own reporting and analytics.
- [Core Developer pricing and add-ons](https://help.elationhealth.com/core-developer-pricing.md): What Core Developer costs, how provider seats are counted, why there are no usage-based charges, and the capabilities you can add on request.
- [Get started with Core Developer](https://help.elationhealth.com/core-developer-access.md): Request a free Elation sandbox, generate API credentials, make your first API call and Hosted Database query, and understand what it takes to reach production.
- [Core Developer FAQ](https://help.elationhealth.com/core-developer-faq.md): Answers to the questions Elation's Technical Solutions and Support teams field most often about Core Developer: pricing, credentials, the sandbox, the APIs, Webhooks, and the Hosted Database.

### Elation Billing

- [Authorization](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/authorization.md): Query prior authorizations issued by payers - look up authorized units, validity dates, and the claims billed against each authorization.
- [Bill](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/bill.md): Query the EHR-side bill a practice generates from a visit note - its billing status, the providers on it, and the Elation Billing claim it produced.
- [Charge](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/charge.md): Query Elation Billing charge line items - the per-CPT lines on a claim, with their charges, adjustments, payments, and outstanding balance.
- [Charge DX](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/charge-dx.md): Report on the diagnosis codes attached to each Elation Billing charge line.
- [Claim](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/claim.md): Query Elation Billing claims to see what was submitted to payers, the providers and authorizations attached, and the EHR-side bill each claim corresponds to.
- [Claim Note](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/claim-note.md): Query the notes billers leave on Elation Billing claims, including who wrote each note and when.
- [Claim Queue](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/claim-queue.md): See which claims were queued for submission to the clearinghouse, when, and by which biller.
- [Clearinghouse transactions and messages](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/clearinghouse-transaction.md): Clearinghouse submission, response, and message records from Elation Billing - a claim's lifecycle through the clearinghouse and the detail returned at each step.
- [Elation Billing User](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/elation-billing-user.md): Identify the billers, administrators, and staff who work claims in Elation Billing.
- [Elation SSO User](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/elation-sso-user.md): Resolve the Elation Billing user on claims, payments, and financial transactions back to the Elation EHR user.
- [ERA Matched Adjustment](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/era-matched-adjustment.md): Query electronic remittance advice charge-level adjustments to analyze payments, denials, and adjustments for Elation Billing customers.
- [Financial Transaction](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/financial-transaction.md): Query the most granular billing financial movements in Elation Billing - the payments and adjustments applied to each charge and claim.
- [Insurance eligibility check](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/insurance-eligibility-check.md): Insurance eligibility verification checks from Elation Billing, with their results.
- [Legal Entity](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/legal-entity.md): Query the Elation Billing legal entities - the tax-ID-holding billing groups a practice bills claims under.
- [Patient Guarantor](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/patient-guarantor.md): Look up the responsible party (guarantor) recorded for a patient in Elation Billing.
- [Patient insurance eligibility](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/patient-insurance-eligibility.md): Insurance eligibility and coverage detail from Elation Billing, keyed to a patient's insurance.
- [Patient Statement Tracking](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/patient-statement-tracking.md): See how many billing statements a patient has been sent and when the most recent one went out.
- [Payer Address](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/payer-address.md): Look up the addresses a practice mails paper claims to for each payer.
- [Payment](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/payment.md): Query insurance and patient payments recorded in Elation Billing - amounts, dates, and how they apply to claims.
- [Payment Note](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/payment-note.md): Query the notes attached to Elation Billing payments, including who wrote each note and when.
- [Tag](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/tag.md): Query the Elation Billing tags applied to claims, patients, and other billing entities.
- [Tag System](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/tag-system.md): Query the Elation Billing tag catalog - the tag definitions (label, color, type) a practice can apply to claims and patients.
- [User Practice](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/user-practice.md): Look up which practices each Elation Billing user belongs to.
- [User Role](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/user-role.md): Look up Elation Billing role definitions for users in your practice.
- [Worklist](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/worklist.md): Query Elation Billing biller worklists to see which claims are flagged for review and their outstanding balance.
- [Worklist Reason](https://help.elationhealth.com/articles/hdb/worklist-reason.md): Query the reasons Elation Billing claims were placed on a biller worklist.
